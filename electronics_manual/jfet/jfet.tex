\section{A Brief Introduction to a JFET Transistor}
\label{lab_jfet}

\bigskip

A \textit{Junction Field Effect Transistor,} or JFET, is very different from the BJT transistors you have seen before.  Although both are three-terminal devices in which a current between two of the leads is controlled by a smaller signal at a third lead, they behave differently enough that their terminals have different names:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item The JFET's  \textit{drain} is analogous to a BJT's collector.
\item The JFET's  \textit{source} is analogous to a BJT's emitter.
\item The JFET's  \textit{gate} is analogous to a BJT's base.
\end{itemize}
