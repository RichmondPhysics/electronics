\begin{Exercise}
In the circuit below, the Zener diode has $V_F=0.7$~V and $V_R=3.2$~V. (a)~Find the instantaneous current through resistor $R_1$ at the time of the peak positive source voltage ($+5$ volts). (b)~Find the instantaneous current through resistor $R_1$ at the time of the peak negative source voltage ($-5$ volts).  Hint: One way to analyze the circuit in the case that you assume you know $V_\text{diode}$ is to treat the diode as a known voltage source (a battery) and use the principle of superposition as you did in problem~\ref{prob_superposition}.
 \end{Exercise}
\begin{Answer}
(a) 2.5 mA (b) $-3.1$~mA.  Hint: if you got 2.27~mA for part (a), you probably assumed that there is current through the diode, so that the voltage across it is 3.2~V.  But as you learned in problem\ref{prob_first_diode_resistor_with_steps}, it's also possible that there is no current through the diode.  One of these scenarios leads to a physically plausible solution, and the other does not; you'll need to try both to see which one is correct.
\end{Answer}
